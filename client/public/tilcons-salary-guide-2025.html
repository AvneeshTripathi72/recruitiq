Insights

Key Hiring Trends 2025

What every employer and job seeker needs to know heading into the year ahead.

AI is reshaping job requirements

Across every sector, familiarity with AI tools is becoming a baseline expectation. Candidates who demonstrate AI proficiency command 15–25% salary premiums.

Skills-based hiring is accelerating

More employers are dropping degree requirements in favour of demonstrated skills, portfolios, and certifications — widening the talent pool significantly.

Speed wins the best talent

Top candidates are typically off the market within 10 days. Companies with streamlined hiring processes have a decisive edge in securing high-quality hires.

Hybrid work is now standard

72% of professionals rank hybrid working as a top-3 consideration when evaluating a new role. Rigid in-office mandates cost employers top talent.

Salary expectations have risen

Candidates in all sectors are benchmarking against current market data and expecting 10–20% increases when switching roles. Underbidding leads to dropouts.

DEI is a competitive differentiator

Organisations with visible DEI commitments attract 40% more applications and see higher offer acceptance rates, particularly among Gen Z candidates.

Best Practices

7 Tips to Hire Better in 2025

Actionable advice from Tilcons consultants based on 250+ successful placements.

  • 1

    Define the role precisely before posting. Vague job descriptions attract the wrong candidates and waste everyone's time. List must-have vs. nice-to-have skills clearly.

  • 2

    Move fast at every stage. Delay a day and you risk losing your best candidate. Aim to give feedback within 24 hours after each interview round.

  • 3

    Benchmark your offer before making it. Use this guide to ensure your compensation is at or above market — especially for high-demand roles.

  • 4

    Sell the role and the company. The best candidates have options. Articulate your culture, growth opportunities, and benefits clearly and compellingly.

  • 5

    Consider total compensation. Salary is just one element. Flexible working, learning budgets, ESOPs, and healthcare benefits are major decision factors.

  • 6

    Involve a specialist recruiter early. Access to a pre-screened talent pool cuts your time-to-hire by up to 60% and significantly improves quality of hire.

  • 7

    Structure your onboarding. The first 90 days are critical. A structured onboarding programme increases retention by up to 82% in the first year.
